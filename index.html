About RTSS

A Legacy of Learning,
Rooted in Palpa

Established in 2025 BS (approx. 1968 AD) under a tree at Rukhmooni Chautara, Shree Ramtulasi Secondary School has grown from a handful of students into a respected institution serving the community of Rampur-5, Bejhad Bazar, Palpa, Nepal. Today we offer ECD through Higher Secondary education with four streams: Science, Management, Education, and Agriculture (CTEVT).

Rich 57-Year History

From 2025 BS beginnings to Higher Secondary status in 2064 BS — a journey of community dedication.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Our management systems meet internationally recognized quality standards.

CTEVT Agriculture Program

Diploma in Agriculture (Plant Science) — a unique vocational pathway since 2071 BS.
